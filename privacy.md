# Privacy Policy for WinterBot

Effective date: 2026-10-17

This Privacy Policy explains how Leif Erickson ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, shares, and protects personal data when you interact with WinterBot ("the Bot") on Discord.

1. Data controller
Leif Erickson
Contact: wintercrew@tuta.com

2. Data we collect
We collect the minimum data needed to operate the Bot and provide features. This may include:
- Discord user ID and username (to identify you and deliver responses).
- Messages and command content you send to the Bot (only when you interact with commands or direct messages).
- Interaction logs (timestamps, command usage, error logs).
- Bot-specific data you provide (settings, preferences, content you upload).
- Technical data from the hosting provider (server logs, IP addresses) only as necessary for operation and debugging.
We do NOT collect or store your Discord password or OAuth tokens. We won't request your password.

3. How we use your data
We use data to:
- Provide and maintain the Bot’s features and respond to commands.
- Store settings and preferences you choose.
- Diagnose and fix technical issues; monitor and improve performance.
- Prevent abuse and enforce rules.

4. Legal basis
Where applicable, our legal basis for processing is:
- Consent (for optional features where you opt-in).
- Legitimate interest (operation, security, and improving the Bot).

5. Data sharing and third parties
We may share data with:
- Discord (as the platform) under their terms.
- Hosting providers, database providers, analytics, or logging services (only as needed to operate or debug the Bot).
We do not sell your personal data.

6. Retention
We keep data only as long as necessary:
- Command logs and interaction logs: 90 days unless needed for troubleshooting.
- Account/preferences data: until you request deletion.
If you want data deleted sooner, contact wintercrew@tuta.com.

7. Your rights
Depending on your jurisdiction you may have:
- The right to access the personal data we hold about you.
- The right to request correction or deletion.
- The right to object to processing or request restriction.
To exercise these rights, contact wintercrew@tuta.com. We may need to verify your identity.

8. Data security
We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect data (encrypted storage for sensitive data, access controls, regular updates). However, no system is perfect — if a breach occurs we will notify affected users and authorities as required.

9. Children
The Bot is not intended for children under 13 (or the minimum age in your jurisdiction). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Changes to this policy
We may update this policy. We will post the updated effective date at the top. Significant changes will be announced on the Bot’s Discord server or via the Bot.

11. Contact
If you have questions or requests about this policy, contact:
Leif Erickson
Email: wintercrew@tuta.com
